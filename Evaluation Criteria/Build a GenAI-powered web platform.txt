Build a GenAI-powered web platform that helps users detect, understand, and verify potentially AI-generated or manipulated images, audio, and video before they trust or share them.

The solution should analyze uploaded or linked media, identify potential signs of AI generation or manipulation, explain the reasoning behind its assessment in an understandable manner, and provide source-traceable context to help users verify the authenticity and credibility of the content.

The platform should empower journalists, researchers, and everyday citizens to make informed decisions in an era where synthetic and misleading media can spread rapidly.

## 🚀 Submission & Evaluation

### 📦 Submission

* *GitHub Repository:* Submit the *`.git` repository link* with your project source code.
* *Deployment:* Deploy your solution on *any suitable platform* such as Vercel, Netlify, Google Cloud, etc.
* *LinkedIn:* Share your project and briefly explain how it addresses the given problem statement.
* *Attempts:* You get *2 submission attempts*.
* After each submission, you receive *evaluation feedback* to help improve your solution.

### 🏆 Evaluation

Your solution will be evaluated on:

* *Code Quality*
* *Security*
* *Efficiency*
* *Testing*
* *Accessibility*
* *Problem Alignment*

### ⚡ Scoring Rule

* If you submit *only once → your 1st submission score counts*.
* If you submit *twice → your 2nd submission score counts*, *even if it is lower than your 1st score*.
* Once the submission window closes, no further attempts are allowed.

> *Build → Submit → Analyse → Improve → Submit. Choose your second submission wisely. ⚡*
